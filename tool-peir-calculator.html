    The PEIR ladder

    Stack the letters P, E, I, R top to bottom. Going down, divide. Going up, multiply. When the letter you need sits between two you know, divide the top by the bottom.

    You knowFindFormula
    P and EIP ÷ E
    E and IRE ÷ I
    I and REI × R
    E and IPE × I
    P and IEP ÷ I
    E and RIE ÷ R
    P and RI√(P ÷ R)

    E is voltage (electromotive force) and I is current (from the French intensité). Watts equal volt-amps only for resistive loads like heaters; motors and electronics draw more VA than watts.
