How conduit fill works

Add up the cross-section area of every wire (NEC Chapter 9, Table 5), then compare it with the conduit’s inside area (Table 4). The limit depends on how many wires there are:

Number of wiresMax fill
153%
231%
3 or more40%

This tool covers EMT with THHN/THWN-2 conductors. Count an insulated ground wire as one of the wires. A bare ground has a smaller area (Table 8), so this tool slightly overstates the fill for bare grounds. For other conduit types or insulations, use the matching Chapter 9 table or Annex C.
