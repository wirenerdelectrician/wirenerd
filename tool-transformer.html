    The formulas

    Single-phase: I = kVA × 1,000 ÷ V  ·  Three-phase: I = kVA × 1,000 ÷ (1.732 × V)

    Use line-to-line voltage for three-phase (480, 208). These are full-load currents. Overcurrent protection for transformers is sized by NEC 450.3, which uses percentages of these currents.
