Wire Color Codes (US)

Common US color conventions for each system, and which colors the NEC actually requires.

SystemPhase / hotNeutralGround
120/240 V single-phaseBlack RedWhiteGreen/bare
120/208 V three-phase wyeBlack Red BlueWhiteGreen/bare
277/480 V three-phase wyeBrown Orange YellowGrayGreen/bare
120/240 V three-phase high-leg deltaBlack Orange: high leg BlueWhiteGreen/bare

What the NEC actually requires

ConductorRule
Neutral (grounded conductor)White, gray, or three white or gray stripes on another color, not green (200.6). Most 6 AWG and smaller must be identified this way along its whole length.
Equipment groundBare, green, or green with yellow stripes (250.119).
High leg (delta)Orange, or another effective marking, wherever the neutral is also present (110.15).
Phase / hot conductorsNo specific color is mandated. When a building has more than one voltage system, the identification method must be posted at each panel (210.5(C), 215.12(C)).

Black-red-blue and brown-orange-yellow are long-standing conventions, not national code requirements. Some local codes and job specs require them. Always check your job specs and the panel directory, and never trust a wire’s color without testing it.
