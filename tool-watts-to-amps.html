Watts ↔ Amps Calculator

Convert watts to amps, or amps to watts, for DC, single-phase or three-phase circuits.

1 for heaters & lamps

    The formulas

    SystemAmpsWatts
    DCI = P ÷ EP = E × I
    Single-phaseI = P ÷ (E × PF)P = E × I × PF
    Three-phaseI = P ÷ (1.732 × E × PF)P = 1.732 × E × I × PF

    For three-phase, E is the line-to-line voltage (208, 240, 480). Power factor is 1 for resistive loads like heaters and incandescent lamps. Motors and electronics are lower, so use the nameplate value when you have it.
