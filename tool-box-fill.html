TOOLS · BOX FILL

Box Fill Calculator

Check whether your wires and devices fit in a box, using the NEC 314.16 counting rules.

1 · The box

cubic inches (use the volume marked on the box if it has one)

2 · Conductors

Count each insulated wire that enters the box and ends or is spliced inside. A wire that passes through unbroken counts once. Pigtails that never leave the box don’t count.

3 · Grounds, devices & fittings

switches, receptacles

    The counting rules (314.16(B))

    ItemCounts as
    Each conductor entering the box1 × its size
    All ground wires (up to 4)1 × largest ground, plus ¼ for each ground beyond 4
    Each device yoke (switch, receptacle)2 × largest wire connected to it
    Internal cable clamps (all together)1 × largest wire in the box
    Each fixture stud or hickey1 × largest wire in the box
