    How derating works

    1. Start with the Table 310.16 ampacity in the column for the wire’s insulation rating (usually 90°C for THHN).
    2. Multiply by the ambient correction factor (310.15(B)) if the ambient isn’t 30°C (86°F).
    3. Multiply by the adjustment factor (310.15(C)(1)) if more than 3 current-carrying conductors share a raceway or cable: 4–6 = 80%, 7–9 = 70%, 10–20 = 50%, 21–30 = 45%, 31–40 = 40%, 41+ = 35%.
    4. The result can’t exceed the ampacity in the column of your terminations’ rating (110.14(C)).

    Neutrals that only carry unbalanced current and equipment grounds don’t count as current-carrying (310.15(E), (F)). Raceways on rooftops in sunlight and other special cases have extra rules.
