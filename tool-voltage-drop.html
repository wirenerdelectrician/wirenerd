    How it’s calculated

    Single-phase: VD = 2 × K × I × L ÷ CM  ·  Three-phase: VD = 1.732 × K × I × L ÷ CM

    K is the resistance of one circular-mil foot of wire (12.9 for copper, 21.2 for aluminum), I is the load in amps, L is the one-way length in feet, and CM is the wire’s area in circular mils from NEC Chapter 9, Table 8.

    The NEC informational notes recommend no more than 3% drop on a branch circuit and 5% total for feeder plus branch. They’re recommendations, not requirements, except where a section or your AHJ makes them mandatory.
