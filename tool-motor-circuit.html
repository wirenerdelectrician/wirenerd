    How motor circuits are sized

    1. Full-load current (FLC): use the NEC table value (430.248 single-phase, 430.250 three-phase), not the nameplate, for wire and short-circuit protection (430.6(A)(1)).
    2. Wire: at least 125% of the FLC for a single motor (430.22).
    3. Branch-circuit short-circuit and ground-fault protection: the device can’t exceed the Table 430.52 percentage of FLC. If that doesn’t land on a standard size, you may go up to the next standard size (430.52(C)(1) Exception 1).
    4. Overload: size from the nameplate FLA: 125% for a service factor of 1.15 or more, or a temperature rise of 40°C or less, and 115% for all others (430.32(A)(1)).

    Values are for standard squirrel-cage induction motors (Design B and similar). Wound-rotor, DC, Design B energy-efficient (Table 430.52 lists separate values) and multi-motor circuits have other rules. Small wire limits in 240.4(D) don’t apply to motor circuits (240.4(G)).
