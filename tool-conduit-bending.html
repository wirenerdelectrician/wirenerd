                These are standard EMT hand-bender field rules. Benders vary, so check the take-up and marks printed on yours, and test on scrap before a critical run.

                Why the numbers work

                Multiplier: the distance between the two bends of an offset is the offset depth ÷ sin(angle). For 30°, sin 30° = 0.5, so the multiplier is 2. A 6" offset needs bends 12" apart.

                Shrink: bending the pipe up and over uses some of its length, so the pipe "shrinks" toward the obstruction. Add the shrink to your measurement so the offset lands where you want it.

                Take-up: a 90° bend uses pipe length too. Subtract the bender’s take-up from the stub height to find where to put your mark.
